Staff FAQ

Before you
sign up.

A quick look at how the team works and what we expect.

Is this a paid job?

Not right now. These are volunteer community roles. We'll always be upfront about compensation, and we won't ask you to do paid work under a volunteer label.

How much time do I need?

Most roles ask for 4–6 hours a week, spread across the times that suit you. Moderators may be asked to cover specific windows once the team grows.

Do I need to be 18?

Moderator and developer roles require applicants to be 18 or older. Community, QA, builder, and support roles are open to applicants 16 and older.

What happens after I apply?

We review applications weekly. If your application moves forward, we'll reach out through Discord or email for a short conversation about the role, expectations, and your availability.

Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes. Pick the role you want most in the form and mention the others in your motivation. We'll figure out the best fit together.

More of a creator?

Make something loud.

If your strength is clips, streams, or edits, the creator program might be a better fit. Same community, different toolbox.
