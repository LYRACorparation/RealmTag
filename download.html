Sideloading 101

From download
to first tag.

You don't need a PC to play, but a PC with SideQuest or adb makes installation easier. Pick the path that fits your setup.

Download the APK

Tap the download button above on your headset browser, or download the file to your PC and transfer it later. Keep the filename as game.apk.

Enable Unknown Sources

On your headset, open Settings → Apps → Unknown Sources and allow your file manager or SideQuest to install apps. Developer Mode is not required for every install method, but it helps.

Install the APK

Use SideQuest's "Install APK" button, run adb install game.apk, or open the file in a headset file manager and confirm the install.

Launch RealmTag

Find RealmTag in Unknown Sources or your app library. Open it, calibrate your height, and jump into a lobby.

Learn the trees

Start with Classic Tag. Swing with your arms, grab ledges, and don't panic when someone drops from above. It happens to everyone.

Safety note: VR movement can be intense. Clear your play space, use your headset's boundary system, and take breaks if you feel dizzy. Don't play near stairs, windows, or pets with strong opinions.

Download FAQ

Before you
hit install.

A few things players ask before their first session.

Is the APK safe?

Only download the file from this page or the official community Discord. We don't ask for your account password, payment details, or headset credentials. If a link asks for any of those, it isn't us.

Will this work on Quest 1?

Quest 2, Quest 3, and Quest Pro are the supported headsets. Quest 1 is not officially supported and may have performance or compatibility issues.

Do I need a PC?

No. You can download and install directly on the headset with a file manager. A PC with SideQuest or adb is simply the most reliable option if you run into trouble.

Why does my headset warn me about the APK?

Android shows a warning for apps installed outside the store. That's expected for sideloaded builds. Confirm that you trust the source before installing.

How do I update?

Download the newest APK and install it over the existing version. Your settings should carry over. If you hit an issue, uninstall the old build first and reinstall.
