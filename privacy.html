1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how RealmTag ("RealmTag", "we", "us", or "our") handles personal information when you visit the RealmTag website, submit a rating, apply to the creator program, apply for a staff role, or otherwise interact with us.

RealmTag is a free, fan-made VR game and is not affiliated with Another Axiom or Gorilla Tag. This policy applies to the website and the forms we operate, not to third-party platforms such as Discord, your headset platform, or your internet provider.

2. Information you provide

We collect the information you choose to submit through our forms. This may include:

  • Ratings: your gamertag (optional), star rating, favorite mode, written feedback, and whether you allow anonymous sharing.
  • Creator applications: your name, email address, Discord username, platform, channel or profile URL, audience size, content focus, and pitch.
  • Staff applications: your name, email address, Discord username, age range, timezone, role preference, weekly availability, relevant experience, motivation, and optional references.
  • Correspondence: any information you send us by email or through community channels when you contact us.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive personal information through these forms. We do not need it, and we do not want it.

3. Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, our hosting provider may collect standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, requested URL, and timestamps. This information is used for security, troubleshooting, and understanding aggregate traffic.

We do not currently use advertising trackers, cross-site behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics that profile you across websites. If that changes, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent.

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • read and respond to ratings and feedback;
  • review creator and staff applications;
  • contact you about an application or community opportunity;
  • operate, maintain, and improve the website and the game;
  • keep the community safe and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests; and
  • detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your application information to send marketing emails unless you separately agree to receive them.

5. Legal bases

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases for processing personal information:

  • Consent: when you voluntarily submit a rating, application, or other information, and when you agree to be contacted about an application.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate and improve the Service, keep the community safe, and respond to inquiries.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law and lawful requests.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that happened before the withdrawal.

6. How we share information

We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Discord: form submissions are delivered to our team through a Discord webhook. Discord processes and stores those messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting providers: our website and APK are hosted by third-party infrastructure providers that process technical data on our behalf.
  • Team members: authorized RealmTag team members may access submissions to review ratings, applications, and reports.
  • Legal and safety: we may disclose information if required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect the safety of players or the public, or to investigate abuse.
  • Business changes: if the project is transferred or reorganized, information may be transferred as part of that change, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Retention

We keep information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Ratings and feedback are generally kept for up to 24 months so we can spot trends and follow up on recurring issues.
  • Creator and staff applications are generally kept for up to 12 months. If you join the team, relevant records may be kept longer for team administration.
  • Technical logs are kept for a shorter period, typically 30–90 days, unless needed for security investigations.
  • We may keep limited records longer if required by law or to resolve a dispute.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner by contacting us. Some records may be retained where we have a legal obligation or a legitimate need to keep them.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we handle, including access controls and encrypted connections. However, no website, webhook, or internet transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your information has been compromised, contact us right away at support@realm-tag.example.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, do not submit information through the website or forms.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it. Users between 13 and 18 should have permission from a parent or guardian before submitting information.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • request a portable copy of information you provided; and
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, contact us at support@realm-tag.example. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

11. International transfers

RealmTag and the third-party services we use may process information in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States. Those countries may have different data protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses offered by our service providers.

12. Cookies and local storage

The website does not currently use advertising cookies. We may use your browser's local storage to remember a short cooldown after you submit a form, which helps prevent accidental duplicate submissions. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

Third-party services we link to, such as Discord, may set their own cookies or similar technologies. Their use is governed by their own policies. You can clear local storage through your browser settings at any time.

13. Third-party links

The website may link to third-party websites, communities, or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content. Review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, provide a notice in the community. Your continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.

15. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information? Contact us at support@realm-tag.example or reach out in the community Discord.

Plain-language summary: we receive what you type into our forms, send it to our team through Discord, use it to review ratings and applications, and don't sell it. Ask us if you want it corrected or deleted.
