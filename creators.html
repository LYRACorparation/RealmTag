Creator FAQ

Before you
apply.

The short version: be real, be kind, and make something you'd want to watch.

Do I need a minimum follower count?

No. We review the person and the content, not just the number. A small channel with a clear voice is just as interesting to us as a large one.

What content performs best?

Gameplay clips with a strong hook, funny fails, creative edits, and beginner-friendly tutorials all do well. Make the movement look fun and the community feel welcoming.

How long does review take?

We review applications weekly. If you don't hear back within 10 days, feel free to follow up in the community Discord.

Is the program paid?

The program is currently perk-based: early access, creator lobbies, featured content, and a community role. We'll be clear about any future paid opportunities before asking for anything.

Can I apply again?

Yes. If your content or channel changes, send a new application. Just don't spam the form — one thoughtful application is better than five rushed ones.

Not a creator?

Help run the realm.

Moderators, builders, testers, and developers keep the jungle healthy. If that sounds like you, check the staff page.
