Is RealmTag free?

Yes. RealmTag is free to download and play. There are no paid stat boosts or pay-to-win items. Cosmetics are unlocked by playing.

Is this Gorilla Tag?

No. RealmTag is an independent, fan-made VR game inspired by the social tag genre. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Another Axiom or Gorilla Tag.

What do I need to play?

An Android-based VR headset such as Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, or Quest Pro. You'll need to enable Unknown Sources and sideload the APK. A PC is helpful for installing, but not required once the game is on your headset.

How do I install the APK?

Download the APK, enable Unknown Sources on your headset, then install it with SideQuest, adb, or a file manager. Our install guide walks through each step.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Private lobbies let you spin up a room and invite your crew. Public lobbies are the quickest way to meet new players.

How do I report a bug or a player?

Use the community Discord or email us at support@realm-tag.example. Include your headset model, what happened, and any steps that reproduce the issue.
